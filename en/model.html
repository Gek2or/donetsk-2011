Reconstruction

How Zarichna is being rebuilt: the scale of the project, the 3D quarter in your browser, a walk down the street, the October 2011 panoramas and the street plan.

01Project scale

From a single facade to a city of a million

The project grows in rings. First a single house checked against a panorama, then a playable corridor, a block, a reference map of the centre and finally all of Donetsk. Every ring shares the same coordinates, so nothing has to be rebuilt.

02The quarter in 3D

Fly over Zarichna yourself

Real project data, assembled right in your browser: 189 OSM footprints, SRTM terrain and the streets of the 280 × 660 m quarter. Rotate, zoom in and click any house to see what is known about it and what is still an estimate.

Your browser does not support WebGL, so a still render of the quarter is shown instead.

Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · Shift or right button to pan · pink numbers open the 2011 panoramas

Data: 189 footprints © OpenStreetMap (ODbL), NASA SRTM terrain, OSM lines as of July 2011. Everything comes from the project's quarter.json file. The detailed geometry of the no. 33 study exists only in Unreal.
