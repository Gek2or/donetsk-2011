Fly over Zarichna yourself

Real project data, assembled right in your browser: 189 OSM footprints, SRTM terrain and the streets of the 280 × 660 m quarter. A Street View camera stood on this spot in 2011.Buildings, streets and terrain come from OpenStreetMap and SRTM. The tram line on Dzerzhinsky Avenue comes from OpenStreetMap: two tracks on their own bed between the carriageways and an overhead wire. In 2011 routes 9 and 10 ran here with Tatra T3SU cars; they are shown in the factory red-and-cream livery. On Zarichna at houses 25–38 wall and roof colours, roof shapes, fences, gate colours, the verge and the gas pipe follow the notes taken from the October 2011 panoramas. Elsewhere fences, trees, poles and windows are typical of Donetsk's private-house streets but not checked against a photo of each house; the cars on the streets are illustrative.
