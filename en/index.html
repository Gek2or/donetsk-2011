DONETSK2011

I have not been to Donetsk since 2012. So I am rebuilding it as it was in October 2011. Every street, house and fence is reconstructed from sources: OpenStreetMap, SRTM radar terrain and Google Street View panoramas from that year. I start with the Voroshylovskyi district, where I grew up, and build the whole city.

A city I cannot go back to

I was born in Donetsk in 1995. My whole childhood was spent in the Voroshylovskyi district: the courtyards, the streets, the way from home to school. It is my home ground, a place I know from memory rather than from maps.

In 2012 I left. In 2014 war came to the city, and the way back was closed. I have not been there since.

For me, Donetsk 2011 is the only way to go back: to rebuild the city as it was, from photographs, maps and panoramas, without inventing anything.

I start with my own district, but I am building all of Donetsk. This city matters to more people than me. There are thousands like me.

Stanislav, creator of the project

A city out of reach for millions

Since 7 April 2014, Donetsk has not been under Ukrainian control. On 30 September 2022, Russia declared it Russian territory. The international community does not recognise the annexation.

The war in Donbas displaced millions of people. For many of them, as for me, the way home is closed. The city still exists, but it cannot be reached physically.
