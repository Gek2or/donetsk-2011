01Goal

What I am building

Not a game inspired by Donetsk but the city itself: living Donetsk in 2011 with a layer of memory on top. Five pillars decide what the game will be.

This project is not a way to pass the time. In essence, it is a missing part of me.

I am sure that a great many people still consider this city their own and remember it the way I do. It is a part of them. A part that exists but is physically out of reach.

Stanislav
The main goal

“This is not a city inspired by Donetsk. This is Donetsk.”

After 20–30 minutes of play, a person should feel two things at once: “I am in the real Donetsk” and “this is a living open world, not a museum”.
Layer 1

Living Donetsk 2011

A systemic open world: traffic, vehicles, residents' daily routines, an economy, the city's reactions and missions. Combat is not the core of the game. Real geometry comes before level-design convenience.

Layer 2

The memory layer

“Memory Echoes”: flashbacks to earlier years at real places on Zarichna Street. The city seen through the eyes of someone who grew up there.

Five pillars of the game
  1. Live in the real cityWalk, take the tram and the marshrutka, buy bread, sit on the bench.
  2. The city lives without youResidents have homes, jobs and routines. Yards keep their own rhythm through the time of day and the weather.
  3. Freedom with consequencesThe world notices what you do through witnesses, reputation and the militsiya, and responds in proportion.
  4. Memory as discoveryReal places unlock memories. Exploring the city is how you find the personal story.
  5. Small, human stakesFamily, friends, neighbours, work and the district. No criminal empires.
02Method

Nothing is invented

Every detail carries an evidence class. If a window is not visible in a panorama, it is not in the model. If a size is estimated by eye, the data says so. That keeps the reconstruction honest as it grows to the scale of a city.

Visible in the October 2011 panorama: material, colour, shape, number of windows.

Development rules
  1. Invent nothing. Every detail has a source and a reliability class from A to F, or X.
  2. Real geometry comes before level-design convenience.
  3. Google and Yandex imagery is reference only and never goes into the game.
  4. Sound and music are made by hand, without AI.
  5. The city grows in rings: Zarichna Street, the Voroshylovskyi district, the neighbouring districts, all of Donetsk. Finished work is never redone.
  6. The unknown stays labelled. An estimated height is never passed off as fact, and a house number or sign appears only with a grade C source or better.
03Engine

From prototype to Unreal Engine 5.8

Zarichna was first assembled in a quick Unity prototype. The project now lives in Unreal Engine 5.8.3 with C++, while the models stay editable in Blender. Coordinates, the source register and the build scripts carried over unchanged. The whole early Unity and Blender history is preserved in an archive branch.

Unreal Engine 5.8

The main platform of Donetsk 2011. Opening the project starts the playable L_ZarechnaQuarter map, and you can already walk through it. City systems (residents, traffic, vehicles, interiors) are tested separately in a synthetic test district and stay out of the reconstruction until there are sources for them.
