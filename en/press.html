About the project

In short

Donetsk 2011 is a solo open-world reconstruction of Donetsk exactly as it was in October 2011, built in Unreal Engine 5.8 from OpenStreetMap, terrain data and street panoramas of that month.

In full

Stanislav was born in Donetsk in 1995 and left in 2012. Since 2014 the way home has been closed. Donetsk 2011 is his way back: a reconstruction of the city as it was in October 2011, the month a Google Street View car drove down Zarichna Street. Every street, house and fence is rebuilt from sources: OpenStreetMap outlines, NASA SRTM terrain and the October 2011 panoramas, used only as visual reference. Every estimate is marked as one. Work starts on Zarichna Street in the Voroshylovskyi district, where he grew up, and grows ring by ring to the whole city. The project website runs in four languages with a live 3D model of the quarter, a photo archive, a memory map and an open update feed.
