Readers’ photos

Do you have your own photos of Donetsk from 2008–2012?

Streets, courtyards, stops, shops, schools: anything shot before 2013. Photos like these help rebuild the city accurately, and the best ones will appear in the quiz and the archive under your name.

  • Only photos you took yourself
  • No close-ups of other people or car number plates
  • Published under CC BY-SA 4.0 with your name as author

04Memory map

The city we remember

Hundreds of thousands of people remember Donetsk. Mark a place you remember on the map: a yard, a school, a tram stop, a bench. Write a few lines. After review your memory will appear here, next to the others.

Click the map to choose a place

Send by email ✉Via GitHub ↗

No account needed: “Send by email” opens your mail app with a ready-made letter. If you have GitHub, you can post there instead, where it is public straight away. Everything reaches the map after review.
