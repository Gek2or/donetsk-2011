    03Tram No. 1

    Ride across the city on tram No. 1

    Route 1 ran from the railway station through the centre, past the Donbass Arena and Lenin Square, to the steelworks: 11.2 kilometres and 26 stops. The tram follows the real track, speeds up, slows for the curves and waits at the stops. Take a window seat.

    04October 2011

    Donetsk in October 2011

    The reconstruction is frozen in a single month, and here it is day by day: weather from the city station, matches, the first frost and the clock change. Facts only, each with its source.

    8.0 °Cmonthly mean, the norm is 8.5 °C
    62 mmof rain, almost twice the 34 mm norm
    +24.1 °Cwarmest day, 7 October
    −4.9 °Ccoldest night, 27 October

    Day by day

    1. 2 Oct
      Football

      Shakhtar beat Zorya Luhansk 4–1 at home, with two goals from Yevhen Seleznyov. 30,804 people watched.

      Source: sport.ua ↗
    2. 7 Oct
      City

      The Champions League trophy stood on Lenin Square for three days. Luís Figo and Dwight Yorke brought it to the city.

      Source: UNIAN ↗
    3. 7 Oct
      Weather

      The warmest day of the month: up to +24.1 °C in the afternoon.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    4. 12–13 Oct
      Weather

      A cold front: 23 mm of rain in two days, and the daily mean fell from 14.7 to 8.8 °C.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    5. 15 Oct
      Time

      Mid-month: sunrise at 6:47, sunset at 17:41. The sun in the 3D quarter follows this day.

      See this sunset in the 3D quarter →Source: sunrise-sunset.org ↗
    6. 16 Oct
      Ice hockey

      HC Donbass beat Toros Neftekamsk 3–0 at the Druzhba Arena in front of 3,155 spectators.

      Source: sport.ua ↗
    7. 18 Oct
      Weather

      The wettest day of the month: 18 mm of rain, gusts up to 43 km/h.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    8. 18 Oct
      Time

      Ukraine’s parliament restored the switch to winter time that it had cancelled a month earlier.

      Source: Lenta.ru ↗
    9. 19 Oct
      Football

      Champions League at the Donbass Arena: Shakhtar 2–2 Zenit, goals from Willian and Luiz Adriano. 50,578 spectators.

      Source: UEFA.com ↗ · Wikipedia ↗
    10. 23 Oct
      Football

      Shakhtar lost 1–2 at home to Metalist in front of 45,204. It was Metalist’s first league win over Shakhtar since 2004.

      Source: LB.ua ↗
    11. 24 Oct
      Weather

      The first frost of the autumn: −0.8 °C at night.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    12. 26 Oct
      Weather

      The coldest day of the month: a mean of −1.6 °C and no more than +1.6 °C by day.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    13. 26 Oct
      Football

      Ukrainian Cup in Donetsk: Metalurh beat Kryvbas 4–0.

      Source: LB.ua ↗
    14. 27 Oct
      Weather

      The coldest night of the month: −4.9 °C.

      Source: NOAA GSOD · Tutiempo ↗
    15. 30 Oct
      Time

      Clocks went back to winter time, from UTC+3 to UTC+2. On 29 October the sun set at 17:16; on the 31st, at 16:13.

      Source: Lenta.ru ↗ · sunrise-sunset.org ↗
    16. All month
      City

      The airport was getting ready for Euro 2012: the new 4 km runway had been in use since July, and the new terminal was still being built. It would open on 14 May 2012.

      Source: UNIAN ↗ · RIA Novosti ↗

    05Memory map

    The city we remember

    Hundreds of thousands of people remember Donetsk. Mark a place you remember on the map: a yard, a school, a tram stop, a bench. Write a few lines. After review your memory will appear here, next to the others.

    Click the map to choose a place

    No account needed: “Send by email” opens your mail app with a ready-made letter. If you have GitHub, you can post there instead, where it is public straight away. Everything reaches the map after review.
